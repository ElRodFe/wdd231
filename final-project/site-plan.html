Overview

Site Name

AutoCare

This name represents clearly a site were the user can take care of its own vehicles and have in hand useful data

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to have a place were vehicle owners can add and keep a track of their vehicle's services and costs to clearly remember usefull information

Scenarios

  • I just started using AutoCare. How can I register my vehicle so I can begin keeping track of its maintenance history?
  • I own more than one vehicle. Can I register all of them in AutoCare and keep their maintenance records organized separately?
  • I'm a vehicle owner who has already registered my car. How can I quickly access the maintenance and repair history of my vehicle?
